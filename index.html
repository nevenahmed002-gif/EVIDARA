The challenge

Rare-disease development needs local evidence.

For rare and genetically driven diseases, clinical-development decisions depend on knowing the patient population, relevant sites, biomarkers, treatment pathways and real-world context in the target market.

Patient visibility

Finding and characterizing eligible patients can be difficult when populations are small and distributed across specialist centers.

Trial feasibility

Protocol design requires realistic assumptions about population size, eligibility, sites and recruitment.

Evidence continuity

Clinical, laboratory and real-world evidence often sit across different systems and research workflows.

The solution

One evidence layer across the development journey.

EVIDARA is designed to help biopharma, biotech, diagnostics and research organizations answer Saudi-specific evidence questions — starting with services and evolving toward a platform.

How EVIDARA reaches patients

Future hybrid and decentralized research models may combine specialist clinical hubs with permitted remote activities such as telemedicine, selected local/home visits, sample collection and patient-reported outcomes — subject to protocol, regulatory and institutional requirements.

Future infrastructure

Patient Passport

A future patient-controlled, consent-driven research profile designed to help patients decide what information can be shared for appropriate research purposes.

Potential evidence

Medical history, diagnoses, medications, laboratory results, biomarkers, imaging summaries, prior treatments and — where appropriate — genomic information.

Patient control

The concept is designed around consent, governance and controlled research use. It is not intended to replace a medical record.

Why now

Saudi Arabia is building a stronger environment for biotechnology and clinical research.

EVIDARA is being developed around this broader opportunity while keeping its own claims focused on the evidence capabilities it can actually build and validate.

Clinical research

Growing clinical-trial activity creates demand for local feasibility and patient-population intelligence.

RWD / RWE

Formal frameworks and institutional initiatives create opportunities for governed real-world evidence work.

Biotechnology

National biotechnology ambitions create a broader ecosystem for advanced therapies and translational research.

Market and policy signals describe the external environment; they are not EVIDARA revenue forecasts, market-size claims or guarantees of data access.

From service to platform

EVIDARA can begin with high-value evidence services and progressively turn validated workflows into software. This reduces the need to build national-scale infrastructure before product-market and partnership assumptions have been tested.
