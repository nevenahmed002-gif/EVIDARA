01 — The Problem

Rare disease research is not only a science problem. It is an evidence-access problem.

Patients can be few, geographically dispersed and clinically complex. Researchers need reliable evidence. Sponsors need feasibility. Healthcare systems need better visibility across the patient journey.

⌬

Fragmented Evidence

Clinical, laboratory, genomic and outcome information can exist across different systems and institutions.

◌

Hard-to-Reach Patients

Rare disease populations are small and may be distributed across different regions of the Kingdom.

↗

Trial Feasibility

Sponsors need to understand whether the right patients, sites and capabilities exist before launching a study.

⌁

Evidence Beyond the Trial

Longitudinal real-world evidence can help answer questions that a conventional trial alone cannot fully address.

05 — Patient Passport

One patient. One longitudinal evidence story.

The future of research should not see a patient as a collection of disconnected visits.

EVIDARA envisions a consent-driven patient research profile that can connect relevant clinical history, laboratory evidence, biomarkers, treatments and outcomes — where appropriate and under the required governance.

The patient doesn't become the product. The evidence becomes the value.
Patient Passport → Evidence Layer → Trial Intelligence → RWE
06 — Reach the Patient

Research should not always require the patient to travel.

EVIDARA is designed to bring appropriate parts of the research journey closer to patients across Saudi Arabia through remote engagement, home-based activities and local healthcare partnerships, where permitted by the study protocol and applicable regulations.

Remote Screening Digital pre-screening and engagement
Home Visits Appropriate protocol-based activities
Sample Collection Laboratory evidence closer to patients
Telemedicine Remote specialist interaction
Remote Monitoring PROs, wearables and digital signals
Specialist Centers Connected research hubs

From Riyadh to remote communities — geography should not determine whether a patient's story can contribute to research.

EVIDARA aims to connect patients with research without trying to replace existing healthcare infrastructure.

Instead, we envision working with healthcare providers, research centers, laboratories and approved home/virtual-care pathways.

15 — Founder Story

Built from a belief that every patient story matters.

EVIDARA started with a simple question: What if we could make the patient more visible in the future of clinical research?

My journey began in medical laboratory science, where I learned that behind every laboratory result there is a patient, a clinical story and a decision that may change a life.

As my journey moved into clinical research, I became increasingly interested in a bigger question: What if the information we already have about patients could be connected, understood and transformed into better evidence?

Rare diseases made this question even more important. When patients are few, geographically dispersed and clinically complex, every piece of reliable evidence matters.

EVIDARA is my attempt to turn that vision into a platform — starting in Saudi Arabia, with a long-term ambition to build a new evidence infrastructure for rare diseases and advanced therapies.
